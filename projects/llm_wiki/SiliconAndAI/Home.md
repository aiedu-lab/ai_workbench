# Silicon and AI — Knowledge Graph

Welcome to the personal knowledge base for the subject
**Silicon Scaling and the Evolution of Artificial Intelligence**.

(transistors, microprocessors, GPU computing) shaped the
trajectory of AI research from the 1940s to the present.
