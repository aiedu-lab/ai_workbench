# Warren McCulloch (1898–1969)

American neurophysiologist and cybernetician. Co-authored with
[[Walter Pitts]] the 1943 paper "A Logical Calculus of the Ideas
Immanent in Nervous Activity" — the first mathematical model of a
[[Neural Networks|neural network]].

## Contribution
The McCulloch-Pitts (MP) neuron modelled a biological neuron as a
threshold logic unit: fire if the sum of inputs exceeds a threshold.
The paper showed that networks of such units could compute any logical
function — establishing a theoretical foundation for [[Neural Networks]]
and inspiring all subsequent work, including [[Frank Rosenblatt]]'s
[[Perceptron]] and [[Marvin Minsky]]'s SNARC machine.

## Historical Position
The MP neuron predated practical digital computers. It was a
theoretical model, not a running program. The hardware to implement
the ideas at scale did not exist until [[Moore's Law]] decades later.
