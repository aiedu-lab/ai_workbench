# Yann LeCun (b. 1960)

French-American computer scientist; Chief AI Scientist at Meta.
Pioneer of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and deep learning.

## Key Contributions
- **Convolutional Neural Networks (1990):** Applied CNNs at Bell
  Labs to recognize handwritten digits using backpropagation.
  The system was deployed widely in the 1990s reading zip codes
  and personal checks — the first genuinely useful real-world
  application of [[Neural Networks]].
- **LeNet (1998):** Refined CNNs into the architecture that
  would eventually inspire [[AlexNet]] (2012).
- **ImageNet and the 2012 watershed:** The convolutional
  approach LeCun pioneered became dominant after [[AlexNet]]
  won ImageNet with a massive margin.
- **Turing Award (2018):** Shared with [[Geoffrey Hinton]] and
  Yoshua Bengio — the "Godfathers of Deep Learning."

## Hardware Context
LeCun's 1990 digit-recognition system worked but was limited
by 1990s hardware. His work demonstrated the architecture was
sound; [[Moore's Law]] scaling over the following 20 years
delivered the GPU compute necessary for it to shine.
