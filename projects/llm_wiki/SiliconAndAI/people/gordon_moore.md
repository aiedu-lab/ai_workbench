# Gordon Moore (1929–2023)

Co-founder of Fairchild Semiconductor and Intel.
Articulated what became known as [[Moore's Law]] in a 1965
article in *Electronics* magazine.

## Key Contributions
- **1965:** Published "Cramming More Components Onto Integrated
  Circuits" — predicted transistor doubling per year (revised
  to every two years in 1975).
- **1968:** Co-founded Intel with Robert Noyce — the company
  that would realize the law bearing his name for decades.
- **1975:** Revised the forecast at the IEEE International
  Electron Devices Meeting; outlined enabling factors including
  MOS technology, die size increases, and finer dimensions.
- **2001:** Co-founded the Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation.

## Moore's Law as Industry Road-map
[[Moore's Law]] became a self-fulfilling prophecy — a goal
the semiconductor industry set for itself. This drove the
exponential growth in compute that enabled:
- [[Deep Learning]] (2012 [[AlexNet]] breakthrough)
- [[Large Language Models]] (2020s)

Moore himself noted (2005): "It can't continue forever. The
nature of exponentials is that you push them out and
eventually disaster happens." He predicted physical limits
within 10–20 years — largely correct in hindsight.
